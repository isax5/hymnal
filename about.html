About the app

Hymns made for everyday worship.

The Adventist Hymnal app was built to put the full hymnal — words, music and audio — into the hands of anyone who wants to worship, practice or lead, in their own language and on their own device.

The story

What started as a simple way to carry the hymnal in your pocket grew into a complete companion for worship. Instead of juggling a printed hymnal, separate sheet music and a music player, everything lives in one place — searchable, offline, and beautifully organized.

What's inside

  • Piano sheet music — full scores for every hymn, crisp on any screen.
  • Instrumental & sung audio — a built-in player for practice or singing along.
  • Lyrics & verses — clean, readable text with adjustable font size.
  • Favorites & lists — save hymns and build sets for services and rehearsals.
  • Search & history — jump to any hymn by number or title in seconds.
  • Offline ready — download once and use it anywhere, online or off.

Built for four languages

The same experience is fully translated across Spanish, English, Russian and Portuguese — so the hymnal feels at home wherever it's used.
